The Anatomy of Koh-i-Noor Boski

Cultivated from double-twisted filament silk, woven to exact 10-pound traditional yard specifications. Designed to be tailored once, cherished for generations.

Boski remains the apex of traditional South Asian gentlemen's tailoring. Our Koh-i-Noor weave is spun exclusively from Grade A Chinese mulberry cocoon silk filaments. Utilizing traditional high-warp count methods, the yarn undergoes precision double-twisting which imbues the material with its legendary 10-pound density.

Unlike synthetic polyester blends masquerading as Boski in open markets, genuine Tanabana spun silk is completely breathable, adapts to body warmth during pleasant winter evenings, and wicks perspiration effortlessly during ceremonial summer celebrations. With each gentle wash, the fabric fiber relaxes, gaining a progressively richer pearl luster.

Filament Count: 60/2 Pure Silk
Fabric Feel: Butter-Soft, Fluid Gravity
Origin Certification: Authenticated Spun

To ensure impeccable fall and drape, please provide these guidelines to your darzi (master tailor):

  • Pre-Shrinkage Soak: Immerse the 4.5m fabric in lukewarm water for 45 minutes prior to cutting. Do NOT wring or twist. Air-dry flat in shaded quarters. Natural silk undergoes an initial 1.5% to 2% consolidation.
  • Needle Specification: Require your tailor to utilize sharp Organ/Schmetz Microtex needles (Size 11/75 or 12/80) to prevent fiber pulling or runs.
  • Interfacing / Bukram: Use premium German soft-fused collar and placket interlinings. Avoid stiff cardboard bukram which contradicts the fluid elegance of spun silk.
